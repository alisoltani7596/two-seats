# Data licensing

These files are generated by `tools/assemble.py`. Do not edit them by hand.

They are **not** all under the repository's MIT licence. Two different licences
apply, because they come from two different places.

## `titles.json` — CC0

Every field in it comes from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/): titles,
years, type, genres, directors, cast, original language, country, runtime,
season counts, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores, IMDb identifiers, and the
Wikipedia language-edition count used as the reach signal.

Wikidata's structured data is released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) — public domain.
You may reuse it for anything, with no attribution required. The app credits
Wikidata anyway, because it is the decent thing to do and it tells people where
to go when a fact is wrong.

The mood tags are derived locally from Wikidata genre labels and from the
Wikipedia summaries; the rules are in `tools/assemble.py`.

## `extra.json` — contains CC BY-SA 4.0 material

The `h` field holds one-line summaries adapted from the opening sentences of
English Wikipedia articles. Wikipedia text is
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), which requires
**attribution** and **share-alike**: a work built on it has to carry the same
licence.

If you reuse this file, or anything derived from those summaries, you must:

1. Credit Wikipedia and link back to the articles. The `w` field holds each
   title's article name for exactly this reason, and the app renders it as a
   link under every summary.
2. License your derivative under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible licence.

The `p` field (story and filming locations) comes from Wikidata and is CC0; it
only shares a file with the summaries for loading reasons.

## `world.js`

World outlines from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), which
places its data in the public domain, via the `world-atlas` TopoJSON build.

## Not included: IMDb

IMDb's downloadable datasets are licensed for "personal and non-commercial
use", which does not cover republishing derived data on a public site. Nothing
here is built from them, and the pipeline never downloads them. IMDb ids are
present only as link targets and come from Wikidata's `P345` property.

See the "The data, and why there is no IMDb rating" section of the root
[README](../README.md).
